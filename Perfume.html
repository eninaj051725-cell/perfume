ABOUT US

More Than a Scent,

It's a Story

At Samouraï Samourai Woman Perfume, we believe that fragrance is more than just a scent—it’s a reflection of who you are, your memories, and your dreams.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to create luxurious, long lasting perfumes that inspire confidence and make every moment special.

Our Story

Samouraï is a historic and highly popular fragrance line originally launched in 1995 by the famous French actor Alain Delon.

Named after Delon’s iconic 1967 film Le Samouraï, the line was inspired by the discipline, honor, and strength of the Japanese warrior.

While it originated as a French designer collection, it evolved into a major cultural phenomenon in Asia—particularly in Japan.
